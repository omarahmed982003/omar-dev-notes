## A stopping point, not another prerequisite chain

Before moving into larger algorithms, try a small quantity, discount, or sum problem yourself. State the input, predict the result, test a boundary, and explain one failure. Continue [PHP fundamentals](/en/php/) until arrays, functions, conditions, and loops are familiar; return to the next path with those tools.

The solved example is not the independent attempt. Try before opening a folded answer, and compare both the output and your reasoning.

## How to complete a lesson

Identify its contract: allowed inputs and required output. Follow a small worked case, then a different independent exercise. An algorithm name is not a solution, and finishing this guide does not certify mastery.
